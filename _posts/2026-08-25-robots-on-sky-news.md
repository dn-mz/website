Amidst the impressive (and to some, frightening!) robot videos, I talked to Sky News about the World Robot Games and what the developments in robot hardware and software mean. We spoke about real world use cases, warfare, safety, governance, the deployment gap... and the day when robots will iron clothes.

Am particularly excited at The Robot Company to bring frontier hardware and software closer to real workplaces. Robots in society, for humanity - those are robots worth building and deploying.
